# Third-party materials (template)

When you publish a mod, list here anything **not** covered by your mod license — especially:

- **Katana ZERO** game assets (property of **Askiisoft** / licensors). See [docs/COMMUNITY_AND_GAME_IP.md](docs/COMMUNITY_AND_GAME_IP.md).
- Other people's mods, extracted bytecode, or art/audio you did not create.

This scaffold file is a reminder; replace or expand it before release.
